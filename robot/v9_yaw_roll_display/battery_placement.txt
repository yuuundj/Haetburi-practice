v9_yaw_roll_display: where to carry the 300 g battery + U2D2 (the display itself is fixed in the hands)
built with HAETBURI_ELECTRONICS=belly|chest|backpack, analysed with sim/analyse.py, sim/walk.py (peak <= 70 % of XL430 stall), sim/loads.py

placement   CoM height  sole behind/ahead of CoM  fore/aft tip  best walk (12 s)                     XL430 worst peak / RMS
belly       191 mm      43 / 42 mm                12.4 deg      419 mm, side   3 mm, turn 17 deg      63 % / 25 %   (hip yaw)
chest       196 mm      43 / 42 mm                12.2 deg      419 mm, side  -9 mm, turn 17 deg      68 % / 25 %   (hip yaw)
backpack    196 mm      35 / 50 mm                10.3 deg      447 mm, side  97 mm, turn -6 deg      69 % / 35 %   (hip yaw)

chosen: belly (unchanged).  The display pulls the CoM 15 mm forward, which lands it over the middle of
the sole (v8 had it 28 mm from the heel and 57 mm from the toe), so no counterweight is needed; moving the
battery back only undoes that and raises the hip loads.
